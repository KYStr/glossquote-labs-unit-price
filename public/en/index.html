Enter what you paid for each pack set and the size of one pack to compare different formats. Use one currency throughout. The tool does not look up products, convert currencies, or infer discounts you did not enter.

Example

Use one currency for this comparison. This tool does not convert currencies. Enter the total price paid for the pack set and the size of one pack.

Price: 0–9,999,999.99, up to two decimal places; no thousands separators.

Size: Greater than 0, up to 1,000,000; up to three decimal places.

Packs: An integer from 1–1000. For two 500 g packs, enter a pack size of 500 and a count of 2.

Keep at least 2 products. You can compare up to 12.

How to enter product details

Compare weight per 100 g, volume per 100 mL, or item count per piece. Results are meaningful only when all prices use the same currency.

How do I compare products with different pack counts?

Enter the size of one pack and the number of packs. The tool calculates the total quantity before comparing the total price.

Should I enter the sale price?

Enter the amount you actually paid for the full pack set. The tool does not infer discounts, tax, or shipping.

Can I compare different currencies?

No. Use one currency for a comparison; this tool does not convert exchange rates.

Can I compare weight and volume together?

No. Choose one category. The tool does not assume a product’s density.

Are my product names and prices uploaded?

No. Calculations stay in the current page’s memory. The result is passed to the browser clipboard only when you select Copy. Leaving or refreshing the page clears your entries.
